📘 Glossary – Key Terms

A

🛡️ Administrative Security

Policies and actions to manage and reduce organizational security risks.

🧼 Anti-Malware

Software to detect and remove malicious programs from systems.

🧠 Awareness Training

Security education programs for employees and users.

B

👁️ Biometric

Authentication using unique physical traits like fingerprints or facial recognition.

🚫 Blacklist

Blocked list of domains, IPs, or users due to malicious behavior.

🔵 Blue Team

Internal defense team protecting systems against cyber threats.

C

🔐 Cybersecurity

Protecting networks and data from unauthorized access and attacks.

🧾 Credential

Login details such as usernames and passwords for access control.

☁️ Cloud Security

Protecting cloud-based infrastructure and data from cyber risks.
